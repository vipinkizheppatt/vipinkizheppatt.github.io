Kizheppatt Vipin




I am a PhD candidate in the Division of Hardware and Embedded Systems within the School of Computer Engineering at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore since September, 2010. My research is supervised by Asst. Professor Suhaib A Fahmy . I am associated with the Centre for High Performance Embedded Systems (CHiPES) lab. I graduated from Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Technology, under Mahatma Gandhi University, Kottayam, India in 2007 with Distinction in Electronics and Communication Engineering. From 2007 to 2010, I worked as an FPGA design and development engineer at Processor Systems India Pvt. Ltd in Bangalore.
